This small, stout bottle vase and its matching cups came through the firing well, and all three have since found separate homes. Together they show how different thicknesses and angles change the way crystals grow within the same glaze.

All three are food safe: the bottle is lined with a standard clear glaze, and the cups with a semi-gloss white.
