A small but pudgy bottle, and one of my favorite examples of how iron and trace oxides can make bright gold crystals on a slightly metallic field. Where the glaze is thicker, at the shoulder, it shifts toward a grayish slate blue.

Sold at the Parrish Art Museum's winter market, with thanks to the Clay Art Guild of the Hamptons.
