Part of a small series exploring 3D printing with grogged stoneware. I don't mind throwing with grogged clay, but I wanted to see how the printer would handle it.

The printed layers aren't perfect, and that's what I find most interesting about them. Beneath the glaze there's a beautiful but precarious wobble.
